The comprehensive architectural roadmap dictates a sophisticated deployment strategy for a system comprised of **65 distinct functional agentic units**, which are meticulously organized into **24 specialized teams**. This architecture is realized through a structured, six-stage evolutionary process that incrementally builds capability from foundational infrastructure to advanced novel discovery.
### Phase 0: Infrastructure Implementation
The genesis of the system, **Phase 0**, establishes the Bureaucratic Infrastructure, a foundational layer consisting of **3 agents** designed to facilitate essential management and communication protocols. This phase instantiates the **Agent Management System (AMS)**, the **Directory Facilitator (DF)**, and the **Agent Communication Channel (ACC)**, creating the necessary digital fabric upon which all subsequent agents operate.
### Phase 1: Establishing the Cognition Core
Following the infrastructure setup, **Phase 1** focuses on constructing the "Cognition" layer, introducing **6 agents** to form the primary reasoning loop. This phase operationalizes four critical teams:

**Problem Analysis Team:** Deploys **2 agents** to deconstruct incoming queries.
**Central Nervous System (CNS):** Deploys **1 agent** to orchestrate overall processing.
**Pilot Solvers:** Deploys **1 agent** for initial solution pathfinding.
**Verification Core:** Deploys **2 agents** to ensure the immediate validity of reasoning.
### Phase 2: Mathematical Domain Expansion
**Phase 2** represents the most significant population surge, deploying **18 agents** to constitute the "Body" of the system. This expansion provides deep subject matter expertise across five dedicated mathematical teams:

**Algebra Team:** **4 agents** dedicated to algebraic structures.
**Calculus Team:** **5 agents** handling continuous change and integration problems.
**Linear Algebra Team:** **4 agents** focused on vector spaces and linear mappings.
**Probability & Statistics Team:** **4 agents** for stochastic processes and data analysis.
**Numerical Fallback Team:** **1 agent** ensuring computational robustness when symbolic methods fail.
### Phase 3: Meta-Cognitive Integration
Transitioning to higher-order processing, **Phase 3** introduces **10 agents** focused specifically on "Meta-Cognition," enhancing the system's capacity for validation and long-term memory management. This phase establishes three sophisticated teams:

**Precondition Validation Team:** **4 agents** ensuring necessary logical prerequisites are met.
**Knowledge Management Team:** **3 agents** tasked with organizing and retrieving information.
**Hypothesis Generation Team:** **3 agents** designed to formulate speculative reasoning paths.
### Phase 4: Governance and Adaptation
**Phase 4** implements a "Governance" layer comprising **9 agents** dedicated to managing internal friction and optimizing system behavior through error correction. This phase activates three regulatory teams:

**Conflict Resolution Team:** **3 agents** to mediate discrepancies between solving pathways.
**Failure Analysis Team:** **3 agents** to diagnose and rectify processing errors.
**Meta-Learning Team:** **3 agents** to improve system performance over time based on past operations.
### Phase 5: Operational Optimization
Focusing on efficiency, security, and resource management, **Phase 5** deploys **6 agents** to refine the "Optimization" of the architecture. This phase integrates three teams critical for production-readiness:

**Distillation & Harvest Team:** **2 agents** to compress knowledge and extract insights.
**Hybrid Deployment Team:** **2 agents** to manage versatile execution environments.
**Operational Hardening Team:** **2 agents** to secure the system against vulnerabilities.
### Phase 6: Frontier Discovery
The final evolutionary step, **Phase 6**, targets "Discovery," adding **13 agents** capable of novel research and handling theoretical ambiguity. This phase completes the architecture with five advanced teams:

**Conjecture Generation Team:** **3 agents** for proposing new mathematical theorems.
**Deep Search Team:** **3 agents** for extensive solution space exploration.
**Algorithm Discovery Team:** **3 agents** for identifying new computational methods.
**Undecidability Navigator Team:** **2 agents** for managing uncomputable or paradoxical problems.
**Formal Knowledge Integration Team:** **2 agents** for synthesizing new findings into the established knowledge base.

This phased rollout ensures that upon completion, the system operates as a unified entity of **65 agents**.
