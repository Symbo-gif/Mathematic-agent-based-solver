## Phase 2 Additions (from "A list of Rules.txt")

### Additional Rule Functions Added

#### N-th Term Divergence Test
- `_check_nth_term_divergence()` - Quick pre-check if terms don't -> 0
- Catches: constants, polynomial growth, exponential growth, factorial

#### Symmetry Rule for Integrals
- `_check_symmetry_integral()` - Odd functions over [-a, a] = 0
- Patterns: x, x^(odd), sin(x), x*cos(x), sinh(x), tan(x), x/(x^2+a^2)
- Called early in `definite_integrate()` (line 3557)

#### Log-Power Classification
- `_check_log_power_integral()` - Classifies int_0^1 x^(-p) * log(x)^m dx
- Rule: Converges iff p < 1 (independent of m)
- Replaces vague "singularity detected" with precise classification

#### Enhanced Taylor Series Recognition
- x^n/n! = exp(x) (exponential series)
- (-x)^n/n! = exp(-x)
- x^n/n = -log(1-x) for |x| < 1
- (-1)^(n+1)*x^n/n = log(1+x) for |x| < 1
